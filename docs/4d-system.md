# The 4-D system

The four stages of work, from understanding the problem to delivering a
verified result.

**Scope:** the stages themselves. No skill name, tool, repo path, or
product decision.

## Discover

Understand the problem, gather evidence, and establish the relevant context.

**Answers:** What's broken, missing, or unclear, and what evidence do we have
for it?

## Define

Set the domain language, intended behavior, boundaries, decisions, and
constraints.

**Answers:** What exactly are we building, what's out of scope, and what
result proves it's correct?

## Decompose

Break the defined work into clear, independent, and executable pieces.

**Answers:** How do we split this into pieces small enough to ship and
verify one at a time?

## Deliver

Build, verify, ship, and close out the completed work.

**Answers:** Is it built, tested, merged, and closed out, with nothing left
dangling?
